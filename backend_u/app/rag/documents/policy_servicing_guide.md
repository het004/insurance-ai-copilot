# Policy Servicing Guide

{{INSURER}} — illustrative servicing guide (demo content).

## Renewing your policy

You can renew up to 60 days before expiry. Renewing more than 7 days early earns a 2% early-renewal discount. Your motor vehicle is uninsured from the day after expiry, and Motor TPL is needed to renew your Istimara. Health policies should be renewed without a break so that cover for declared conditions continues. Life contributions are level, so your renewal contribution does not change during the term.

## Renewal contribution changes

At motor renewal the insured market value is reduced for one more year of age, your No Claim Discount steps up if you made no at-fault claim, and tariff revisions may apply. The Copilot shows the current contribution, the renewal contribution and the reasons for the change before you confirm.

## Changes to your policy

Common changes include updating your mobile number, email or National Address, adding or removing a named driver, changing beneficiaries on a life policy, and updating vehicle details after a change of plate. Contact changes are free and effective immediately. Adding drivers or members may change the contribution.

## Iqama renewal

Residents must keep their Iqama valid. If your Iqama has expired, we cannot issue new policies online until it is renewed on Absher and you verify again with Nafath.

## Cancellation and refunds

You may cancel a policy at any time. Motor and general policies are refunded on a pro-rata basis for the unexpired period, provided no claim has been made. Motor TPL cannot be cancelled while the vehicle remains registered in your name unless alternative cover is provided. Health policies for members linked to a residency permit follow the applicable regulations.

## Documents

Your policy schedule is available instantly in the Copilot and the app. Motor policies are linked electronically to the traffic authorities, so you don't need to carry a printed copy.

## Contact and turnaround times

Service requests raised through the Copilot are acknowledged instantly. Standard turnaround: contact changes immediate, beneficiary changes 1 working day, endorsements with a contribution change 2 working days, refunds 7 working days.
