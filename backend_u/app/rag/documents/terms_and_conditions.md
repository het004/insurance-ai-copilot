## General exclusions (motor)

We will not pay for: loss or damage outside the territorial limits of the policy; damage while the vehicle is used for a purpose not declared (for example a private car used for ride-hailing without declaration); damage while driven by a person without a valid licence for the vehicle class; damage while the driver is under the influence of alcohol or drugs; racing, rallies and off-road events; wear and tear, depreciation (except on Motor Comprehensive Plus), mechanical or electrical breakdown; and consequential loss.

## General exclusions (health)

We will not pay for: cosmetic treatment unless needed because of an accident; experimental or unproven treatments; treatment outside the Kingdom except emergencies where the plan provides cover; and conditions that should have been declared but were not.

## General exclusions (life)

We will not pay for: suicide within the first policy year; death caused by pre-existing conditions that were not disclosed; hazardous activities that were not declared; and critical illnesses diagnosed within the first 90 days of cover.

## General exclusions (home)

We will not pay for: wear and tear and gradual deterioration; termites and pests; damage while the home is unoccupied beyond the permitted period; wilful damage; and land value.

## Duty of disclosure

You must answer all questions truthfully and completely, including previous claims, vehicle modifications, chronic conditions and smoking habits. Non-disclosure may result in a claim being declined or the policy being cancelled.

## Fraud

If any claim is fraudulent in any respect, all benefits for that claim are forfeited and the policy may be cancelled without refund. Insurance fraud may be reported to the authorities.

## Data protection

Your personal data is processed only for underwriting, servicing, claims and regulatory purposes, in line with Saudi data protection law. Identity checks through Nafath, Yakeen and the National Address are performed only with your consent. Voice inputs to the Copilot are converted to text in your browser.

## Complaints

Complaints can be raised through the Copilot or any branch. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate the complaint to the Insurance Authority.

## Contribution and VAT

Cover starts once the contribution is received. Contributions include 15% VAT for health, motor and general insurance; life insurance is VAT-exempt.
