# Frequently Asked Questions

{{INSURER}} — illustrative FAQs (demo content).

## Is motor insurance mandatory in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Every vehicle on the road must have at least third-party liability (Motor TPL) cover, and valid insurance is needed to renew the Istimara. Driving without insurance can lead to fines.

## Does my motor policy cover floods and sandstorms?

Motor Comprehensive and Motor Comprehensive Plus cover damage from floods, rain, storms and sandstorms. Motor TPL does not cover damage to your own vehicle.

## Do I need a Najm report?

Yes. For motor accidents we need an accident report from Najm (or Taqdeer, where it applies). Request it at the scene through the Najm app or by calling Najm.

## Will my contribution go up after a claim?

After an at-fault motor claim, your No Claim Discount resets to 0% at the next renewal. Windshield-only claims and claims where the other party is at fault do not affect your discount.

## What is agency repair?

Agency repair means your car is repaired at the official dealer for its brand using genuine parts. It is included in Motor Comprehensive Plus for vehicles up to 5 years old.

## Can I buy insurance with an Iqama?

Yes. Residents register with their Iqama number and verify with Nafath. The Iqama must be valid.

## Is VAT charged on insurance?

Health, motor and general contributions include 15% VAT. Life and Retirement & Savings plans are exempt from VAT.

## Can I cover my family on my health plan?

The Copilot sells individual health plans — each person has their own plan. Participants over 65 need a medical declaration review before the plan is issued.

## Are chronic conditions covered?

Declared chronic conditions such as diabetes and hypertension are covered from day one up to the plan limit, with a 15% contribution loading.

## How much life cover do I need?

A common guide is 10 times your annual income plus any outstanding loans, for a term that lasts until your family no longer depends on your income.

## Can I pay with mada or Apple Pay?

Yes. We accept mada, Apple Pay, STC Pay, credit cards and SADAD.

## Is my home's land value insured?

No. Insure the building for its rebuilding cost and your contents for their replacement value; land is not insured.
