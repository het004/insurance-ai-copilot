# Claim Process Guide

{{INSURER}} — illustrative claims guide (demo content).

## Step 1: Report the incident

Report the incident as soon as possible. You can report through the AI Copilot by saying, for example, "I had an accident" or "I was admitted to hospital", or through the {{SHORT}} mobile app. A claim ID in the format CLM-YYMM-NNNN is generated instantly.

For motor accidents, an accident report from Najm (or Taqdeer, where it applies) is required. Call Najm or use the Najm app at the scene; if there are injuries, call the Red Crescent and the police first.

## Step 2: Documents required

Motor own damage: Najm or Taqdeer report, driving licence of the driver, Istimara (vehicle registration), ID or Iqama, and photos of the damage. Theft: police report, both original keys and the Istimara. Third party: Najm report showing liability. Health: medical card or ID at the hospital's insurance desk; for reimbursement, the medical report and original invoices. Home: police report for burglary, photos and a list of damaged or stolen items. Life: death certificate, the life assured's ID or Iqama, beneficiary ID and bank IBAN.

## Step 3: Assessment

Motor claims are assessed at our approved workshops or, on Motor Comprehensive Plus, at the dealer agency. Smaller claims can be assessed by video self-inspection. Do not start repairs before the estimate is approved.

## Step 4: Approval and settlement

Repairs are paid directly to the workshop or agency; you pay only the deductible (usually SAR 500 for private cars). Reimbursement claims are paid by bank transfer to your IBAN within 7 working days of receiving complete documents. Total-loss and theft claims are settled on the insured market value within 15 working days of receiving all documents.

## Claim status stages

Every claim moves through five stages: **Reported**, **Under Review** (documents verified), **Assessment** (inspection or medical review), **Approved** (settlement confirmed) and **Closed** (payment made). Ask the Copilot "What is the status of my claim?" at any time.

## Health pre-approval

Planned admissions need pre-approval, which the hospital requests from us; decisions are usually given within one hour. Emergency treatment does not need pre-approval in advance.

## What can delay a claim

Missing Najm report, expired driving licence or Istimara, a driver not licensed for the vehicle, repairs started before approval, and incorrect IBAN details are the most common reasons for delay. Claims may be declined if the driver was under the influence or the vehicle was used for an undeclared purpose.
