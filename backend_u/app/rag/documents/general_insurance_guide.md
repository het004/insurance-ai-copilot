## Our home plans

**Home Basic** protects the building — walls, roof, floors and permanent fixtures — against fire, lightning, explosion, flood, rain and sandstorm damage, riots and malicious damage, bursting of water tanks and pipes, and impact by vehicles.

**Home Plus** covers everything in Home Basic plus your contents: furniture, electronics, appliances and air-conditioners. It adds burglary and theft with forcible entry, accidental damage to TVs and appliances, and alternative accommodation for up to 6 months if your home becomes unliveable after an insured event.

**Home Premier** is our complete home protection. It adds jewellery and valuables up to 20% of the contents value, personal liability up to SAR 500,000, alternative accommodation for up to 12 months, personal accident cover for domestic workers, and breakdown of AC units and appliances.

## How much to insure

Insure the building for its rebuilding cost, not its market price — land is not insured. As a guide, an apartment typically costs SAR 400,000–800,000 to rebuild and a villa SAR 1–3 million. Insure contents for their replacement value. If you are under-insured, claims are reduced proportionately.

## How the contribution is calculated

The building is rated at about SAR 0.45 per SAR 1,000 of rebuilding value and contents at about SAR 1.60 per SAR 1,000. Villas carry a 15% loading because they stand alone. Homes in cities with flash-flood exposure, such as Jeddah, Jazan, Makkah, Abha and Al Baha, carry a 10% loading. VAT at 15% is added. The minimum contribution is SAR 250 before VAT.

## Home claims

Report burglary to the police and to us immediately, and share the police report and a list of stolen items. For fire, flood or water damage, take photos before cleaning up and prevent further damage if it is safe to do so. A surveyor visits within 48 hours for claims above SAR 20,000. You can report a home claim to the AI Copilot by describing what happened.

## What is not covered

Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, termites and pests, damage while the home is unoccupied for more than 60 days (90 days on Home Premier), wilful damage, and valuables unless covered under Home Premier.
