## Our health plans

All our health plans include at least the essential benefits set by the Council of Health Insurance, including inpatient and outpatient treatment, maternity and newborn care, emergency treatment and declared chronic conditions.

**Health Essential** gives an annual limit of SAR 1,000,000 per member with access to more than 1,200 hospitals and clinics in our Class C network. Outpatient visits carry a 20% co-payment up to SAR 100 per visit, and inpatient stays are in a shared ward.

**Health Gold** is our most popular plan. It gives an annual limit of SAR 1,000,000 per member, a wider Class B network of more than 2,000 hospitals and clinics including major private hospitals, a private room, a 10% outpatient co-payment, dental cover up to SAR 2,000, optical up to SAR 750 and 24/7 telemedicine.

**Health Premier** is our VIP plan with an annual limit of SAR 5,000,000 per member, access to all hospitals including the VIP tier, no co-payment, a private suite, dental up to SAR 5,000, optical up to SAR 1,500, worldwide emergency cover and second medical opinions abroad.

## Who can be covered

Our online health plans are individual plans for one person aged 18 to 75. The contribution depends mainly on your age band, the plan and your city.

## Chronic and pre-existing conditions

Declared chronic conditions such as diabetes, hypertension and asthma are covered from day one up to the plan limit. A 15% loading applies to the contribution when a chronic condition is declared. Conditions that are not declared may lead to a claim being declined.

## Using your cover

Show your {{SHORT}} medical card or your ID at the insurance desk of any network hospital. Planned admissions need pre-approval, which is usually decided within an hour; emergencies are treated first and approved afterwards. Telemedicine consultations are available 24/7 through the Copilot and our app on Health Gold and Health Premier.

## VAT

Health insurance contributions include VAT at 15%.

## Members over 65

Members over 65 can be covered, but online purchase is followed by a medical declaration review; our team calls within one working day before the policy is issued.
