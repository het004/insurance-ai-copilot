# Retirement & Savings Guide — {{INSURER}}

Our Retirement & Savings plans combine regular monthly saving into Shariah-compliant funds with Takaful protection for your family. They are individual plans for participants aged 18 to 60. Contributions are exempt from VAT. This guide is illustrative content for a proof of concept.

## Our three plans

**Savings Plan** helps you save towards any goal — a home, marriage or Hajj. Contributions go into a Shariah-compliant balanced fund. You can top up any time, pause contributions for up to 6 months, and make partial withdrawals after 3 years. The annual admin fee is 1.5% of the fund.

**Retirement Plan** builds a fund to age 60 using a target-date fund that moves gradually to lower-risk Sukuk and money-market funds as retirement approaches. At retirement you can take a lump sum or a monthly income. Contributions are waived if you become totally disabled. The admin fee is 1.0% a year, and withdrawals before age 55 carry a charge.

**Education Plan** saves for your child's university. If the participant dies during the term, {{SHORT}} keeps paying the remaining contributions so the fund still reaches its target, and the fund can be paid out over four years of study.

## Contributions and terms

The minimum contribution is SAR 300 a month and the maximum online is SAR 50,000 a month. Terms run from 5 years (10 years for the Retirement Plan) up to 30 years, and plans must mature by age 70. Contributions above SAR 10,000 a month need a source-of-funds check before the plan is issued.

## Takaful protection

Every plan includes Takaful protection: if the participant dies during the term, the family receives the protection amount or the fund value, whichever is higher. The protection amount is at least SAR 50,000.

## Projections are not guaranteed

Quotes show illustrative fund values at 3%, 5% and 7% growth a year after fees. Fund values can go down as well as up, and past performance does not guarantee future returns.

## Surrender and withdrawals

Surrendering a Savings or Education Plan in the first 3 years carries a surrender charge. After 3 years you can withdraw part of your fund value without closing the plan.
