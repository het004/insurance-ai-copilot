## Our life plans

**Life Protect** is pure term protection. If you pass away during the term, we pay the full sum covered to the beneficiaries you choose. The contribution stays the same for the whole term, and cover applies worldwide, 24/7.

**Life Protect Plus** adds living benefits: 50% of the sum covered is paid on diagnosis of any of 30 critical illnesses (such as cancer, heart attack and stroke), the sum covered is doubled for accidental death, and total permanent disability is covered.

**Life Premier** is complete family protection. It adds a waiver of contribution if you become disabled or critically ill, a family income benefit of 1% of the sum covered each month for 2 years, spouse cover of 25% of the sum covered, and early payment of the full sum covered on diagnosis of a terminal illness.

## How much cover and for how long

A common guide is 10 times your annual income, plus any outstanding loans. Sum covered can be from SAR 100,000 to SAR 10,000,000 online. Terms are 10, 15, 20, 25 or 30 years, and cover must end by age 70. Choose a term that lasts until your children are independent or your home finance is repaid.

## How the contribution is calculated

The contribution depends on your age, the sum covered, the term, whether you smoke and the plan. Smokers, including shisha users, pay a 60% loading. Contributions are level for the whole term.

## VAT

Life insurance is exempt from VAT in Saudi Arabia, so no VAT is added to your life contribution.

## Medical examination

No medical examination is needed online for applicants up to 55 with a sum covered up to SAR 3,000,000. Above that, our team arranges a medical examination at a partner clinic before the policy is issued.

## What is not covered

Suicide in the first policy year, death caused by undisclosed pre-existing conditions, critical illnesses diagnosed in the first 90 days, and undeclared hazardous activities are excluded.

## Making a life claim

Beneficiaries can report a claim through the Copilot or any branch. They need the death certificate, the life assured's ID or Iqama, the beneficiary's ID and bank IBAN. Approved claims are paid within 10 working days of receiving all documents.
