# About {{INSURER}}

{{INSURER}} is a Takaful (Shariah-compliant cooperative insurance) company in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Participants contribute to a shared Takaful fund that pays claims, and any surplus can be shared with participants. This knowledge base is illustrative content for a proof of concept; products, figures and wordings are examples only.

## Our five individual portfolios

Through our AI Copilot you can get a quote and buy in a single conversation, by voice or text, for individuals, in five portfolios. **Health**: Health Essential, Health Gold and Health Premier individual plans. **Motor**: Motor TPL, Motor Comprehensive and Motor Comprehensive Plus for private cars, SUVs, electric cars and motorcycles. **Life**: Life Protect, Life Protect Plus and Life Premier family Takaful protection. **Retirement & Savings**: Savings Plan, Retirement Plan and Education Plan. **General**: Home Basic, Home Plus and Home Premier for apartments and villas. Family, group, business and commercial-vehicle cover, travel and marine are not sold through the Copilot.

## Buying online

New customers register in seconds with their National ID (Saudi citizens) or Iqama (residents). With the customer's consent we send a Nafath authentication request, then fetch identity data from Yakeen and the customer's National Address. Existing customers verify with their registered mobile number, email or policy number. Payment can be made by mada, Apple Pay, STC Pay, credit card or SADAD. Quotes are valid for 15 days and the policy is active from the day of payment.

## VAT

Health, motor and general insurance contributions include VAT at 15%. Life insurance contracts are exempt from VAT in Saudi Arabia.

## Service and complaints

Claims can be reported 24/7 through the AI Copilot or our mobile app. Motor accidents must be reported to Najm (or Taqdeer where applicable). If you are not satisfied with our service, raise a complaint through the Copilot; if it is not resolved, you may escalate it to the Insurance Authority's complaints channel.
