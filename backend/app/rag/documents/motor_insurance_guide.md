## Our motor plans

**Motor TPL** is third-party liability cover, which is mandatory for every vehicle on Saudi roads and required to renew the Istimara (vehicle registration). It pays for bodily injury, death (including diya) and property damage you cause to others, up to SAR 10 million per accident. It does not cover damage to your own vehicle.

**Motor Comprehensive** covers your own vehicle against collision, overturning, fire, theft, floods, storms and sandstorms, plus all third-party liabilities. Repairs are carried out at our approved workshops, and 24/7 roadside assistance and towing are included.

**Motor Comprehensive Plus** adds agency (dealer) repair, no depreciation deducted on replaced parts, a replacement car for up to 10 days, cover when driving in other GCC countries, and personal accident cover for the driver and passengers. Agency repair is available for vehicles up to 5 years old.

## How the contribution is calculated

The own-damage contribution is a percentage of your vehicle's current market value, adjusted for vehicle type, age and the city where it is mostly driven. Riyadh, Jeddah, Makkah, Dammam, Al Khobar and Dhahran are rated slightly higher because of traffic density. The third-party contribution depends on the vehicle class. VAT at 15% is added to the total.

## No Claim Discount

A No Claim Discount (NCD) reduces your own-damage contribution for every claim-free year: 15% after one year, 20% after two, 25% after three and 30% after four or more consecutive claim-free years. The discount resets to 0% after an at-fault claim. Windshield-only claims do not affect your NCD.

## Vehicle details

When you register with us, we can fetch your vehicle details from the official registration record using your plate number (for example 7788 BDR) or the 9-digit sequence number on your Istimara, so you don't need to type them in.

## Electric vehicles

Electric vehicles are covered under all our plans. Battery packs are expensive to replace, so we recommend Motor Comprehensive Plus for agency repair. Charging cables are covered as accessories.

## Driving in the GCC

Motor Comprehensive and Motor TPL cover you inside the Kingdom. Motor Comprehensive Plus extends own-damage and third-party cover to the other GCC countries, including crossing the King Fahd Causeway to Bahrain.
